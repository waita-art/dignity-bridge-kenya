Our Impact

Turning Community Action Into Meaningful Change

We are building an approach that focuses on practical opportunities, stronger communities and measurable progress over time.

Our Impact Story

Our impact story is just beginning.

Dignity Bridge Kenya is being built with a long-term focus on people, partnerships and sustainable community development.

As our programmes grow, we will document our activities, reach and outcomes so that our supporters, partners and communities can see what is being achieved.

What We Aim To Change

Four areas of community impact

01

Youth Opportunity

Young people gain access to digital skills, career information, mentorship, life skills and connections to opportunities.

  • Digital skills
  • Career readiness
  • Mentorship
  • Entrepreneurship awareness
02

Healthier & Safer Communities

Communities have access to information, awareness and appropriate referral pathways around health, substance abuse, violence and other challenges.

  • Health awareness
  • HIV prevention information
  • Substance-abuse prevention
  • Violence and crime awareness
03

Dignity for Older Persons

Older persons are supported through initiatives that promote dignity, inclusion, social connection and access to appropriate support.

  • Dignity initiatives
  • Social support
  • Rights awareness
  • Community inclusion
04

Stronger Grassroots Organisations

Community-based organisations become better positioned to organise, develop programmes, mobilise resources and build partnerships.

  • Organisational capacity
  • Resource mobilisation
  • Monitoring & evaluation
  • Partnership development
Measuring Progress

We want to understand what changes.

Impact is more than counting activities. As our work develops, we will track relevant information that helps us understand participation, reach, outcomes and areas for improvement.

Our monitoring approach will help us learn from implementation and make better decisions as programmes evolve.

01

Reach

Who participated and how many people were reached.

02

Participation

How people engaged with activities and programmes.

03

Outcomes

What knowledge, skills, connections or changes resulted from our work.

04

Learning

What we can improve based on evidence and feedback.

We will publish relevant figures and stories when they are supported by our programme records and monitoring systems.
