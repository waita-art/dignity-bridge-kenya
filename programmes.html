What We Do

Programmes designed around people and community needs.

We focus on practical skills, health and prevention, dignity for older persons and stronger grassroots organisations.

Our Work

Creating opportunities. Strengthening local solutions.

Dignity Bridge Kenya combines direct community programmes with partnerships that strengthen local organisations. Our approach is designed to connect people with knowledge, skills, resources and opportunities.

01
Youth Development

Youth Empowerment

Helping young people develop practical skills, access useful information and connect with opportunities.

Digital Literacy

Basic computer skills, internet literacy and access to digital resources.

Career Guidance

Career information, CV preparation, job-search support and interview preparation.

Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship awareness and practical financial literacy.

Mentorship

Leadership, life skills, positive role models and professional connections.

02
Health & Prevention

Healthy and Safe Communities

Providing appropriate information, awareness and referrals that support healthier and safer communities.

HIV/AIDS Awareness

Age-appropriate information on HIV prevention, awareness and appropriate referrals.

Substance Abuse Prevention

Awareness around substance abuse, peer pressure and responsible decision-making.

Violence Prevention

Supporting awareness, conflict resolution and safer community environments.

Healthy Lifestyles

Health information, life skills and mental-health awareness and referral.

Specialist health and other professional services are delivered or supported through qualified professionals and relevant partners.

03
Older Persons

Dignity for Older Persons

We believe growing older should never mean losing dignity, respect or a sense of belonging.

Dignity Drives

Community initiatives responding to identified needs among vulnerable older persons.

Home Visits

Appropriate social support, needs assessment and referrals.

Basic Needs

Support with appropriate food, clothing, hygiene and other identified essentials.

Social Inclusion

Community activities, awareness and intergenerational engagement.

04
Local Organisations

CBO Strengthening

Supporting grassroots organisations with the systems, skills and partnerships they need to strengthen their work.

Organisational Assessment

Understanding governance, programme, financial and organisational capacity.

Proposal Development

Support with project design, proposal development and funding readiness.

Financial Management

Strengthening budgeting, documentation, accountability and financial systems.

Monitoring & Evaluation

Helping organisations track activities, results, evidence and learning.
