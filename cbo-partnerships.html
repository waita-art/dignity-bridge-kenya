CBO Partnerships

Strengthening Organisations That Strengthen Communities

We work alongside grassroots community-based organisations to strengthen their capacity, improve their readiness and build stronger pathways to sustainable community impact.

Why CBOs Matter

Local organisations understand local challenges.

Community-based organisations are often closest to the people and communities they serve. They understand local priorities, relationships and challenges.

Dignity Bridge Kenya seeks to work with grassroots organisations by helping strengthen the systems, skills and partnerships that support effective community development.

Our approach is based on collaboration, practical support and shared learning rather than creating dependency.

🤝

Partnership Over Dependency

We aim to help community organisations become more organised, connected and prepared to access opportunities and deliver sustainable programmes.

How We Support CBOs

Practical support for stronger organisations

Support can be adapted to the needs, stage and priorities of each organisation.

01

Organisational Assessment

Understanding organisational strengths, gaps, priorities and areas where additional capacity may be needed.

02

Governance

Supporting organisations to strengthen internal structures, roles, accountability and organisational practices.

03

Proposal Development

Helping organisations communicate community needs, programme ideas, objectives and implementation plans clearly.

04

Fundraising & Resource Mobilisation

Building knowledge and practical approaches for identifying opportunities, developing relationships and mobilising resources.

05

Financial Management

Supporting stronger budgeting, financial planning, record keeping and responsible use of resources.

06

Monitoring & Evaluation

Helping organisations track activities, document progress and learn from their programmes and work.

07

Safeguarding

Promoting awareness of safeguarding responsibilities and practices that help protect people participating in community programmes.

08

Partnership Development

Supporting organisations to identify and develop relationships with relevant community, professional and development partners.

Our Approach

Building partnerships step by step

Our engagement can begin with understanding an organisation's current situation and identifying practical areas for collaboration.

01

Connect

Learn about the organisation, its community and the work it is already doing.

02

Assess

Identify organisational strengths, challenges and priority areas for support.

03

Strengthen

Work together on practical skills, systems, knowledge and organisational capacity.

04

Connect Opportunities

Where appropriate, support stronger connections to resources, partnerships and opportunities.

Community Mapping

Understanding the grassroots landscape

Effective partnerships begin with understanding who is already working within a community and what they are doing.

Dignity Bridge Kenya can engage with grassroots organisations to better understand local priorities, existing initiatives, available resources and potential areas for collaboration.

Areas we may explore

  • Community needs and priorities
  • Existing programmes and services
  • Organisational capacity
  • Community relationships
  • Resource and training needs
  • Potential collaboration opportunities
