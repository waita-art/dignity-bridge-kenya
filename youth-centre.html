Flagship Initiative

Dignity Youth Digital, Skills and Community Centre

A safe place to learn, connect and grow.

The Vision

More than a computer centre.

Young people need more than access to technology. They need information, guidance, mentorship, practical skills and safe spaces where they can explore their potential.

The Dignity Youth Digital, Skills and Community Centre is designed to bring these opportunities together in one accessible community space.

The centre will combine digital learning, career support, employability training, mentorship, health information, life skills and constructive recreation.

What's Inside

A space built around opportunity.

01

Digital Learning Lab

Access to computers, internet, printing, scanning, digital resources and practical computer training.

  • Basic computer literacy
  • Internet literacy
  • Digital skills
  • Access to online resources
02

Career & Skills Corner

Practical support to help young people understand their options and prepare for opportunities.

  • Career guidance
  • CV preparation
  • Job-search support
  • Interview preparation
  • Entrepreneurship awareness
03

Mentorship & Life Skills

Connecting young people with positive role models and practical life skills.

  • Leadership
  • Mentorship
  • Life skills
  • Professional networking
04

Health Information

Age-appropriate information and appropriate referrals that support healthy decision-making.

  • HIV/AIDS awareness
  • Substance abuse prevention
  • Healthy lifestyles
  • Mental health awareness
05

Recreation

Constructive indoor activities that provide young people with a safe and welcoming environment.

  • Table tennis
  • Chess
  • Drafts
  • Board games
  • Educational games
06

Community Learning Space

A flexible space for workshops, seminars, meetings, mentorship and community learning.

  • Youth workshops
  • Career days
  • Community meetings
  • CBO training
